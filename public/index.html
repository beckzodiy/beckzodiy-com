beckzodiy — my aim

Make sending money home to Uzbekistan cheap and instant.

Using legal stablecoins. Scroll to see why, and how it works.

01 — why

1.4 million Uzbeks work abroad.

In Russia, Kazakhstan, Korea, Turkey, Europe, the US, and here in the UAE. Almost every month, they send money home to their families.

02 — why

$18.9B

was sent home to Uzbekistan in 2025. That money is about 14% of the whole economy.

03 — the problem

The old way is slow and expensive.

Money hops from bank to bank, often through US dollars in New York. Every stop takes a fee and time.

World average cost: 6.36%. Through banks: 14.9%.
Every 1% in fees costs Uzbek families $189 million a year.

step 1 — Dubai

Dirhams become a stablecoin.

You pay AED in a licensed app. You get the same value as a stablecoin: digital money tied 1:1 to a real currency. Example: AE Coin, where 1 coin = 1 dirham.

step 2

Now the money is just a message.

"Send 1,000 to wallet 0x7f…a3", signed with a secret key so only you could have sent it.

A message is just data: ones and zeros.

step 3 — under the sea

It travels as light.

Glass fiber optic cables on the seabed carry it from the Gulf, through the Red Sea and the Suez Canal, into the Mediterranean.

step 3 — over land

Then across land to Tashkent.

Through Europe's internet hubs, then east across Russia and Kazakhstan. This route: about 12,000 km in 0.06 s.

One typical route. Real paths change, because the network picks the fastest one by itself.

inside the cable

Thinner than a hair.

The light bounces inside the glass and never escapes. A flash means 1, darkness means 0.
It moves at about 200,000 km per second.

01000001 01000101 01000100 = "AED"
step 4

Computers around the world check it.

The payment is copied to many computers in many countries. They all agree it's real, so no one can fake it or spend it twice. This takes seconds, not days.

step 5 — Andijan

The family gets soʻm.

A licensed partner in Uzbekistan turns the stablecoin into soʻm, straight onto a Humo or Uzcard card.

old way vs. my aim

Same money. Less lost.

Today, world average 6.36% · can take days
My target under 1% · under a minute

A worker sending $250 a month loses about $190 a year at 6.36%. That's school books, winter coats, a gas bill.

that's my aim

Cheap and instant money home. For every Uzbek family.

It isn't built yet, but the law is ready. The UAE allows licensed dirham stablecoins, and Uzbekistan opened a stablecoin payment sandbox on 1 January 2026.
